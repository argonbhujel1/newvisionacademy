Email & Communication Settings

Email auto-reply setup
Auto-replies only work when SMTP is configured in your .env file: 
MAIL_SERVER=smtp.gmail.com
MAIL_PORT=587
MAIL_USE_TLS=true
MAIL_USERNAME=your@gmail.com
MAIL_PASSWORD=your-app-password
For Gmail, use an App Password, not your normal password. Restart the app after changing .env.
